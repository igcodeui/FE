OPENING / INTRO Step 1

Agent: Hi, this is ( Agent Name )with First Benefit, How are you doing today?

Before we continue, let me quickly verify a couple of details.

Great, and can you please confirm your first and last name for me?

Perfect, thank you, Mr./Ms. (First Name).

Agent: Based on your recent benefits review, it looks like there may have been changes in your situation, lifestyle, or current needs.

BUILD RAPPORT + PERMISSION

Agent: My role is simply to gather a few updated details so we can check what options may now be available to you. You could potentially qualify for lower monthly premiums or better health benefits that are better suited to your current lifestyle and priorities.

No worries at all, this is only a review and information session. There’s absolutely no obligation, and you’re not signing up for anything during the call. Our goal is simply to provide you with updated information and help you understand the options that may fit you best. most.

CURRENT COVERAGE Step 2

Agent: To start, do you currently have any life insurance or final expense coverage in place?

IF YES Path:

Agent: Perfect — honestly, that’s exactly why many people speak with us. A lot of families already have something in place, but policies sometimes become outdated, overpriced, or don’t provide all the benefits available today. What we do is review everything side-by-side to make sure you’re not overpaying and that your family would actually be fully protected if something happened.

FOLLOW WITH:

• Current insurance carrier: _________ (make sure they are talking about life insurance)

• Approximate coverage amount: How much would it pay your loved ones? _________

Agent: It looks like you were at one point paying about 80.00? Is that correct? Yes, ok, no ok what was it, maybe that’s a system error?”
Monthly payment (if willing): _________

IF NO Path:

Agent: Okay, no problem. A lot of people intended to get coverage earlier and just never got around to it, or rates were too high at the time. The good news is we work with multiple top-rated insurance companies, so instead of just one option, we can compare plans and often find some of the lowest available rates based on your situation. We have strategies to offset costs as well.

GOAL-BASED QUESTIONING Step 3

Agent: What’s most important to you with coverage?

Examples:

• Not leaving bills behind

• Burial/funeral expenses

• Protecting family

• Leaving less stress on family, allowing a celebration of your life? (that is what a lot of people have said to me)

• Locking in affordable rates

• Leaving something behind for loved ones in general.

• Remember these plans pay your beneficiary directly via check immediately.

IF THEY HESITATE / TRANSITION

Agent: Totally understandable. Most people I speak with mainly want to make sure their family isn’t left with funeral costs, medical bills, or financial stress later on. And honestly, rates usually don’t get cheaper as we get older, so reviewing options sooner rather than later can really help keep costs down while maximizing benefits.

HEALTH SECTION Step 4

Just a few questions left-

Agent: And are there any health conditions or medical concerns we should be aware of?

Examples:

• Diabetes

• Heart condition

• COPD

• Cancer history

• Stroke

• Medications

IF HEALTH ISSUES:

Agent: I appreciate you sharing that with me. That actually helps us a lot because some companies are much better than others with certain health conditions.

And even if someone has been declined before, we also have guaranteed issue programs available in many cases — so there are usually still options available. That’s one thing our specialists are really good at: solving problems and finding the best fit based on the person, not just the diagnosis.

Lastly, can you please confirm your Date of Birth?

TRANSITION TO LICENSED AGENT Step 5

Agent: Perfect — I’ve got what I need to help point you in the right direction. My job is simply to connect you with a licensed specialist who can review the available options, help maximize benefits, and potentially save you money based on what we discussed. This should only take a few minutes.

CROSS-SELL / HOUSEHOLD INSURANCE SAVINGS (OPTIONAL)

Agent: Also, just to see if there may be additional savings available overall, let me ask you quickly:

• Do you currently have homeowners or renters insurance?

• Auto insurance?

• Medicare or any supplemental coverage?

💡 Critical: Dispo these and let agent know if its auto or home , this bundling will set up for a lowering home and auto by the amount of final expense plan.

TRANSITION

Agent: Perfect. In addition to reviewing your life insurance options, there may also be opportunities to help lower some of your other insurance costs as well. The goal is making sure you and your family are properly protected without overpaying.

LIVE HANDOFF TO AGENT Final Step

Agent: So, (Customer Name), I’m now going to bring in __________, one of our licensed specialists. I’ll stay on briefly to make the introduction smooth and easy for you.

AGENT INTRO / WARM TRANSFER VERBATIM READING:
Agent: Hi, __________, this is (Customer Name). We reviewed their current situation, and they’re interested in exploring affordable protection options. I’ll let you take it from here.
